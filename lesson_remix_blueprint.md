# Lesson Script: Mastering the Remix Tool

## Scene 1: The Hook (Avatar Full Body / Cinematic Office)
**Script:** "Yo, welcome to the lab. Today, I'm showing you exactly how to lock in a flawless digital likeness using our Remix tool. It all starts with one thing: the perfect source photo. If you get this wrong, the AI gets it wrong. Let’s look at the blueprint."

## Scene 2: The Right Way (Avatar Circle Overlay / B-Roll: Good Photo Example)
**Visual:** Show a high-quality medium close-up. Clear lighting, full face visible, including shoulders and upper chest.
**Script:** "This is what we need. A high-res, medium close-up. Notice how the full face is unobstructed—no hats, no shades, no hair blocking the features. We need the shoulders and the top of the chest in the frame. This gives the AI the geometry it needs to build an accurate representation of you from every angle."

## Scene 3: The Wrong Way (Avatar Circle Overlay / B-Roll: Bad Photo Montage)
**Visual:** Montage of blurry shots, side profiles, group photos, and people wearing heavy sunglasses/hats.
**Script:** "Avoid the noise. Blurry shots, group photos where the AI has to guess who you are, or faces half-hidden by shadows and accessories will trash your results. If you can't see your eyes and jawline clearly, neither can the machine. Don't waste your tokens on a bad start."

## Scene 4: The Close (Avatar Full Body / Branding)
**Script:** "Keep it clean, keep it centered, and keep it professional. Grab that sharp headshot and let’s see what you can build. I’ll see you in the next one."
