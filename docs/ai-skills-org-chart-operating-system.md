# AI Skills Studio — Org Chart + Weekly Operating System

Updated: 2026-02-17 (UTC)
Owner: Bobby Newland

## North Star
Build AI Skills Studio to **$1M+ MRR** while generating **immediate cashflow** through consulting + marketing.

---

## 1) Org Chart (Two-Speed Company)

### CEO — Bobby (Final Decision Layer)
- Sets direction, approves offers, approves strategic bets.
- Owns partner/channel relationships and major deal approval.
- Reviews one weekly scorecard; avoids day-to-day execution drag.

### A. Revenue Command (Cash Now) — Priority #1
**Mission:** produce cash this month via consulting/marketing.

#### 1) Sales & Pipeline Pod
- Outbound prospecting (DM/email/calls)
- Discovery + qualification
- Proposal + close workflow

**KPIs:** meetings booked, close rate, cash collected this week

#### 2) Client Delivery Pod
- Onboarding + implementation
- Campaign execution + reporting
- Retention/upsell motion

**KPIs:** fulfillment margin, client retention, avg account value

#### 3) Proof Engine Pod
- Case studies, testimonials, before/after assets
- Offer pages and social proof distribution

**KPIs:** new proof assets/week, win-rate uplift, inbound lead lift

---

### B. Product Command (Scale Engine) — Priority #2
**Mission:** compound into SaaS MRR.

#### 1) Product & Monetization Pod
- Packaging, pricing, feature priority, onboarding journey

**KPIs:** trial→paid conversion, activation rate, ARPU

#### 2) Engineering Pod
- Build and ship retention/growth features
- Reliability/performance hardening

**KPIs:** deploy frequency, incident count, cycle time

#### 3) Growth Pod
- Lifecycle experiments (activation, conversion, referral)
- Paid + organic experiment queue

**KPIs:** CAC payback, conversion lifts, experiment velocity

#### 4) Customer Success/Community Pod
- Habit loops, support quality, community stickiness

**KPIs:** D30/D90 retention, churn, NPS/qual feedback trends

---

### C. Intelligence + Automation Layer (Cross-Functional)
- **Chief of Staff AI (MiniMax):** synthesis + tradeoffs + priority decisions
- **Research Swarm (Kimi):** market intel, pain mining, trend signals
- **Build Brain (Gemini/Claude):** architecture, debugging, high-leverage coding
- **Ops Automation:** cron, storage health, backup, handoff, QA guardrails

---

## 2) Resource Allocation Rule
- **70% effort:** Revenue Command (cash now)
- **30% effort:** Product Command (MRR compounding)
- Rebalance only after stable monthly baseline revenue and predictable lead flow.

---

## 3) Weekly Meeting Cadence (Lean)

### Monday — Strategy + Targets (30 min)
- Confirm weekly cash target and MRR target
- Lock top 3 revenue tasks + top 2 product tasks
- Kill low-leverage work

### Wednesday — Pipeline + Product Check (20 min)
- Revenue: meetings, closes, blockers
- Product: feature progress, activation metrics
- Re-route resources if behind target

### Friday — Scorecard + Decision Review (30 min)
- What shipped, what sold, what learned
- Keep/kill list for next week
- Update case studies and offer positioning

---

## 4) Weekly Scorecard (One Page)

## Revenue Command
- Cash collected (week)
- Meetings booked
- Deals closed
- Delivery margin %
- Churned clients

## Product Command
- New trials
- Trial→paid %
- MRR delta
- D30 retention
- Experiments launched/completed

## Operating Health
- Deploys this week
- Critical incidents
- Storage health status
- Backup status
