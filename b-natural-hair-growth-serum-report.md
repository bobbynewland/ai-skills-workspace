## 2. Key Ingredients for Hair Growth Serum

### Top 10 Ingredients with Scientific Benefits:

1. **Minoxidil** (FDA-approved) - Stimulates blood flow to follicles, extends anagen phase
2. **Rosemary Oil** - Improves circulation, may be as effective as minoxidil
3. **Pepper Extract (Capsaicin)** - Increases blood flow, stimulates IGF-1 production
4. **Biotin (Vitamin B7)** - Essential for keratin production, strengthens hair
5. **Saw Palmetto** - Inhibits DHT production (natural alternative to finasteride)
6. **Caffeine** - Blocks DHT, stimulates hair follicle activity
7. **Copper Peptides** - Promotes collagen, improves follicle health
8. **Zinc** - Essential mineral for hair tissue growth and repair
9. **Iron** - Corrects deficiency-related hair loss (common in Black women)
10. **Niacin (Vitamin B3)** - Improves blood circulation to scalp

### Natural Alternatives (for "Natural" positioning):
- Aloe Vera - Soothes scalp, contains proteolytic enzymes
- Castor Oil - Rich in ricinoleic acid, promotes circulation
- Jojoba Oil - Similar to scalp's natural oils
- Peppermint Oil - Cooling sensation, stimulates blood flow
